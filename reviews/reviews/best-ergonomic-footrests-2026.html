Best Ergonomic Under-Desk Footrests for Posture & Circulation (2026)

Published by ErgoEngine | Updated for 2026

If you experience fatigue, lower back strain, or restless legs after hours at your desk, the missing link in your setup might be your foot support. An ergonomic under-desk footrest promotes active sitting and better lower body alignment.

Why You Need an Under-Desk Footrest

When your feet dangle or rest flat on the floor without proper support, pressure builds up in your lower back and thighs. A quality footrest elevates your legs, improves blood circulation, and reduces spinal compression.

Key Features to Look For

  • Adjustable Height/Angle: Rocking mechanisms or multi-level tilt options to match your height.
  • Surface Texture: Massage bumps or memory foam padding for enhanced comfort during barefoot or shoed use.
  • Stonewall Stability: Non-slip bottom grip to prevent sliding on hardwood or carpet floors.
